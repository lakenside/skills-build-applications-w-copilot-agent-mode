# Activities

This document lists curated activities and challenge ideas for OctoFit Tracker.

## Manga Maniacs

- **Overview:** Manga Maniacs is a themed, short-duration activity designed to combine light physical movement with reading and creativity. It encourages students who enjoy manga and comics to stay active through bite-sized, character-inspired exercise bursts and log them as part of their weekly activity.

- **Goal:** Make short, frequent movement breaks fun for students who love manga, and connect reading interest with physical activity.

- **How it works:**
  1. Students choose a favorite manga character or series.
  2. During a "Manga Break" (5–10 minutes), students perform a short sequence of simple exercises inspired by their chosen character (e.g., "Hero Jumping Jacks", "Ninja Stance Holds", "Speed Steps").
  3. Students log the activity in OctoFit Tracker with the activity type set to `Manga Maniacs` and include the character/series in the notes field.

- **Scoring & Points:**
  - Each Manga Break (5–10 minutes) = 10 points.
  - Extra creativity bonus (+5 points) for including a short description or picture (if allowed) explaining how the sequence relates to the character.
  - Team combo bonus: if 3 or more team members log a Manga Break within the same day, each gets an extra +5 team points.

- **Badges / Achievements:**
  - "One-Punch Starter": First Manga Maniacs activity logged.
  - "Manga Marathon": 10 Manga Maniacs sessions completed.
  - "Team Panel": Participate in a team Manga Maniacs day (3+ teammates).

- **Safety & Accessibility:**
  - Exercises should be modifiable for different fitness levels and accessible to students with mobility limitations (e.g., seated variants).
  - Teachers should provide example sequences and modifications.

- **Sample Week Challenge:** "Manga Week" — students earn a bonus leaderboard multiplier for each Manga Maniacs session logged that week. Encourage themed days (e.g., "Shonen Sprint Wednesday", "Slice-of-Life Stretch Friday").
