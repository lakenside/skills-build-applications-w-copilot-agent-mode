# School Clubs

This page lists extracurricular clubs available to students.

## Manga Club

- **Description:** Dive into the world of Japanese manga—epic heroes, quirky sidekicks, and unforgettable panels. Join fellow readers to explore gripping story arcs, debate iconic moments, and bring favorite characters to life with themed activities, fan-art swaps, and casual cosplay-lite meetups. Whether you're a seasoned otaku or a curious newcomer, Manga Club is a welcoming space to celebrate illustration, storytelling, and creative community.
- **Schedule:** Tuesdays at 7pm
- **Max attendance:** 15 people
- **Notes:** Open to all students. Activities include group readings, character discussions, fan art sharing, short sketch exchanges, and themed social events. Teachers or club leaders should monitor attendance to respect the maximum capacity.
