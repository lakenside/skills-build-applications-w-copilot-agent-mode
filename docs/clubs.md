# School Clubs

This page lists extracurricular clubs available to students.

## Manga Club

- **Description:** Explore the fantastic stories of the most interesting characters from Japanese Manga (graphic novels).
- **Schedule:** Tuesdays at 7pm
- **Max attendance:** 15 people
- **Notes:** Open to all students. Activities include group readings, character discussions, and themed social events. Teachers or club leaders should monitor attendance to respect the maximum capacity.
